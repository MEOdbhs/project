HOME / INTRO

A fixed place is never truly fixed.

This project began as a sound study.
It became a study of attention.

By returning to the same bench every day,
I started noticing more than sound.
I noticed light, shadow, weather, rhythm, strangers, repetition, and my own shifting thoughts.

What looked ordinary at first
became layered through repetition.

The bench stayed the same.
My way of seeing it did not.

ABOUT

About the Project

I returned to one bench for seven consecutive days.

Each day, I arrived at the same time
and stayed for fifteen minutes.

The original task was to document sound,
but the process expanded naturally.

I began recording not only what I heard,
but also what I saw,
who moved through the space,
and what happened in my mind while I sat still.

This project is a record of a place,
but it is also a record of perception.

METHOD

Method

One location.
One time.
Seven days.
Fifteen minutes each day.

Observation Categories

What I heard
Environmental sound, passing conversation, movement, traffic, wind, birds, interruptions.

What I saw
Light, weather, shadows, objects, motion, color, and small physical changes.

Who I met
People who passed by, paused nearby, shared the bench, or appeared repeatedly.

What I thought
Distraction, memory, emotion, boredom, focus, and shifts in awareness.

The goal was not perfect documentation.
The goal was sustained observation.
