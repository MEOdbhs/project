INTERVIEW / 002
Inside the Mind of Jack
BIO
Jack is currently reflecting on identity, emotions, and long-term direction. He often thinks about how to adjust expectations, treat himself with more kindness, and keep moving toward a balanced and grounded life.
I want to treat myself better while staying clear about what matters most at this stage.
THE CONVERSATION
01
What is the one thing you’ve been thinking about the most recently?
I’ve been thinking about who I am now, what I should do, how I should think, and also what I’m going to eat tomorrow 🤔.
02
What is the biggest source of stress in your life right now?
I put too much pressure on myself that doesn’t really belong to me, and my expectations for myself are too high. When I can’t reach them, I overthink a lot and feel a lot of stress.
03
What do you think you are lacking the most right now?
Honestly, I’m not really lacking anything, but maybe I’m missing a stable intimate relationship.
04
How do you usually adjust yourself when you’re in a bad mood?
When I’m in a bad mood, I usually ask my friends to go out with me and relax. Sometimes I play games I like or watch anime to distract myself.
05
What do you think people your age are most likely to feel anxious about right now?
I think it’s still about wanting to get into a relationship too much, not having a clear understanding of themselves, appearance anxiety, and feeling confused about the future.
06
What is the one thing you look forward to most in the future?
After graduating and going back to China, I hope I can buy a car that I really want.
07
What part of yourself do you want to change the most right now?
In the past, I was too strict with myself. I want to treat myself better, and I also want to improve my procrastination.
08
Among love, friendship, and academics, which is the most important to you at this stage?
Academics comes first. As for friendship and love, I don’t think they are necessities for me, more like something that adds balance to life. But if I do have one, I still hope for a stable relationship.
09
Do you have a thought that others may not really understand, but that you care about deeply?
Yes. I always feel that people can never completely understand each other. If they could, there probably wouldn’t be wars in the world. I think a lot about the future and the present, but sometimes my overthinking leads to strange or even childish thoughts. I don’t usually share them with others because I’m afraid they won’t understand me, but I do care about them a lot.
10
If you could say one sentence to yourself from a year ago, what would it be?
Just keep living like this. I’m actually quite satisfied with the life waiting for you in the future.
KEY INSIGHTS
“I put too much pressure on myself, and when I can’t reach my expectations, I start to overthink.”
“Academics comes first for me, while friendship and love feel more like something that adds balance to life.”
“Sometimes I think too much about the future and the present, but I’m afraid other people won’t really understand me.”
“I want to treat myself better and stop being so strict with myself.”
